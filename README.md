## 💼 The Business Problem

Every year, companies lose **billions** due to unexpected employee attrition.

- 📉 Replacing one employee costs **50–200% of their annual salary**
- 🔄 High turnover disrupts team productivity and morale
- ❌ HR teams react *after* employees resign — never *before*

**The core challenge:**
> *"How can HR identify at-risk employees early enough to take action?"*

---

## ✅ How This Project Solves It

| Problem | Solution |
|--------|----------|
| No early warning system | ML model predicts attrition with 86% accuracy |
| Unknown attrition drivers | EDA reveals top factors — Overtime, Salary, Satisfaction |
| No visibility across departments | Power BI dashboard shows real-time workforce health |
| Reactive HR decisions | Data-driven insights enable proactive retention strategies |

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze attrition patterns across departments, roles & salary levels
- Identify key drivers of employee turnover
- Build ML model to flag at-risk employees before they resign
- Deliver insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard

## 🤖 Machine Learning Model

- **Algorithm:** Random Forest Classifier
- **Accuracy:** 86%
- **Goal:** Predict employees with high attrition risk
- **Output:** At-risk employee list for HR intervention

**Top Attrition Factors Identified:**
1. 🔴 Overtime — #1 driver of attrition
2. 💰 Low Monthly Income
3. 😞 Low Job Satisfaction
4. 🚗 High Business Travel
5. 📅 Less Years at Company

---

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

An interactive dashboard built for HR leaders & management:

- 📌 **KPI Cards** — Total employees, attrition rate, avg salary
- 🏢 **Department Analysis** — Attrition breakdown by department
- 👔 **Job Role Analysis** — High-risk roles identified
- 💵 **Salary Analysis** — Income vs attrition correlation
- ⏰ **Overtime Impact** — Overtime vs attrition visualization
- ⚠️ **Employee Risk View** — Individual attrition risk scores
- 🎛️ **Interactive Filters** — Slice by department, role, salary, age

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- Employees doing **overtime are 3x more likely** to leave
- **Sales & HR departments** have the highest attrition rates
- Employees earning **below average salary** show higher exit risk
- **Early career employees** (0–3 years) are most vulnerable to attrition
